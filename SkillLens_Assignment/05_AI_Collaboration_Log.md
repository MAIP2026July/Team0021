# AI Collaboration Log

This log records the AI tools used in preparing this submission, the main areas where they helped, where their output needed correction, and the decisions made by the author.

---

## 1. Tools Used

| Tool | Used for |
|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Drafting and editing written documents, checking them against the course packet, and research on public sources |
| Claude Code (terminal) | Organising files and folders, and extracting text from the course packet |
| Figma (AI design features) | First-pass screen layouts for the wireframes |

---

## 2. Where AI Helped

- **Research:** public sources on Skillsoft's benchmark assessments and published work on LLM-based scoring reliability.
- **Drafting:** first versions of the PRD, metrics, roadmap, UX specification, risk note and economics note.
- **Wireframes:** first-pass screen layouts, from written screen specifications.
- **Checks:** comparing the documents against the course packet's required sections.

---

## 3. Where AI Output Needed Correction

| Issue | How it was handled |
|---|---|
| Generic framing in early drafts | Narrowed to one decision and one first wedge (Python for data analysis) |
| Confidence reported by the model | Replaced with confidence determined by code, from repeated runs and a quotation check |
| Lesson-based remediation | Replaced with rubric-based feedback and follow-up scenarios |
| Mixed submission language ("individual" and "team") | Corrected to an individual submission |
| Model prices and cost inputs not available from a live source | Labelled as illustrative assumptions in the economics note |
| Unverified citations | Authors and titles checked; unverifiable authors removed |
| Styled wireframes rather than low fidelity | Kept as the author's decision |

---

## 4. Decisions by the Author

| Area | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendation | Prototype, then pilot only after the reference-set thresholds are met | The prototype shows the workflow; accuracy has not been measured |
| Scope | Skill-agnostic product, with Python for data analysis as the worked example; hiring and business tracks after the MVP | Keeps the MVP narrow enough to test |
| Second skill | Communication skills, with two scenarios | Tests the skill-agnostic design with open-ended responses |
| Remediation | Feedback and follow-up scenarios on the same objective; micro-courses deferred | Keeps the AI central to the product and measurable |
| Benchmark items | Not scored in the MVP | Benchmark items are multiple-choice and matching; the MVP scores open-ended responses |
| Market position | Skillsoft provides the catalogue, platform and delivery; SkillLens provides open-ended assessment and evaluation | The catalogue already exists; the gap is evidence-based assessment of applied skill |
| Thresholds | Kept as targets to be tested on the reference set before any release | Targets must be tested, not assumed |
| Wireframe style | Coloured screens kept for the submission | Author's decision |

---

## 5. Final Reflection

AI reduced the time needed for research, drafting and layout. The scope, the risk acceptance, the thresholds and the recommendation were decided by the author, and every claim in the submission was checked against its source.

Two figures are cited with their status: the 994% growth figure is company-reported, and the LLM agreement range (0.30 to 0.80) comes from a secondary synthesis.
